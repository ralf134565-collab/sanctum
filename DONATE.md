# Supporting the Development of Sanctum 🍃

Hello! My name is Viacheslav, and I am the creator and sole developer of **Sanctum**.

Sanctum was born as a personal manifesto against the modern internet — a world crowded with ads, expensive subscriptions, attention-grabbing tricks, and the corporate trading of personal data. I wanted to build a completely honest, quiet, and secure vault for your thoughts that runs 100% on your device and treats your mind with gentleness and respect.

The app is **100% free, completely open-source, and has no hidden fees**. Because all processing and AI calculations happen locally on your hardware, we don't have massive server bills. However, writing the code, optimizing on-device LLMs, designing haptic feedback, and maintaining the app take thousands of hours of intense creative labor.

If you believe in our mission of offline, private, and non-manipulative software and want to support its continuation, here is how you can help.
